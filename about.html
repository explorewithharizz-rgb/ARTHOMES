Studio Leadership

Gopinathan K (B.E., MBA.)

With an engineering and business management foundation, Gopinathan K established Art Homes Interior Studio to bring structured reliability, transparent Bill of Quantities (BOQ), and disciplined on-time delivery to residential interior design.

Under his leadership, Art Homes operates on a strict single-point accountability model: our team coordinates design, material sourcing, factory cutting, civil adaptations, and finishing so homeowners enjoy an effortless experience.

Partnering with spatial architect & 3D visualizer Moneesh Kumar Krishnan, our studio creates realistic virtual walkthroughs ensuring every millimeter of your space is verified before on-site carpentry begins.

Methodology

Our 5-Step Execution Workflow

From initial site laser audit to final 140-point quality handover.

01

Site Laser Audit

We visit your site, discuss layout ideas, and measure every wall and conduit with precision lasers.

02

8K 3D Walkthrough

We create full 3D models with true-to-life lighting and material textures so you can preview the finish.

03

Itemized BOQ

Transparent pricing with zero hidden surprises. Every brand and hardware spec is listed in detail.

04

Factory Precision

Panels are machine-cut on automated edge banders, followed by clean on-site assembly by master carpenters.

05

Quality Handover

140-point quality audit, deep cleaning, smooth drawer testing, and issuance of your 10-Year Warranty.
