Our Story & Purpose

Where Indian Heritage Meets German Precision.

Art Homes Interior Studio was created with a clear singular mission: eliminate the unpredictability, hidden cost escalations, and delayed timelines that plague traditional residential carpentry.

By combining state-of-the-art virtual walkthroughs with an in-house 6,000 sq.ft modular manufacturing facility and European hardware, we ensure that every millimeter of your home is pre-verified before on-site installation begins.

We operate on a strict single-point accountability model. From civil adaptations and electrical false ceilings to solid Teak wood turning and quartz countertop fitting, our team manages everything under one roof.

Our Execution Blueprint

The 45-Day Turnkey Journey

A disciplined milestone roadmap from initial laser measurement to final turnkey handover.

01 Days 1 – 7

Site Laser Audit & Virtual Walkthrough

Millimeter-accurate laser room scanning, space layout optimization, and high-resolution virtual walkthrough of every room.

02 Days 8 – 14

Material Curation & Itemized BOQ

Hands-on swatch selection of acrylics, laminates, quartz, and hardware, followed by a transparent, unalterable BOQ contract.

03 Days 15 – 28

Factory Modular CNC Production

In-house German automated edge banding and panel cutting while civil, electrical conduits, and gypsum ceilings progress on-site.

04 Days 29 – 40

Precision On-Site Assembly

Master carpenter assembly of modular carcasses, soft-close Blum hardware, acrylic shutters, quartz counters, and LED cove strips.

05 Days 41 – 45

140-Point Audit & Final Handover

Deep cleaning, drawer alignment tests, handover ceremony, and issuance of your official 10-Year Warranty Certificate.
