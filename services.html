Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries from homeowners regarding our turnkey design and execution process.

How does the 45-day handover guarantee work? +
Once design drawings and material specifications are approved, our 6,000 sq.ft factory cuts and edge-bands all modular panels simultaneously while civil, electrical, and ceiling work finishes on-site. This parallel workflow enables a guaranteed handover in 45 working days.
What grade of plywood is used in your modular kitchens? +
We exclusively use IS:710 certified BWP (Boiling Waterproof) marine grade plywood from Greenply and CenturyPly for all wet areas and kitchen carcasses. This ensures zero swelling or warping even under prolonged water contact.
Do you provide design walkthroughs before we commit? +
Yes! Every project begins with millimeter-accurate spatial planning and photorealistic design views so you can visualize lighting, color palettes, and circulation before any carpentry begins.
What hardware brands do you install in wardrobes and drawers? +
All drawers and cabinets feature European hardware from Blum (Austria) and Hettich (Germany) with soft-close tandem runners tested for 100,000+ open-close cycles.
Are there any hidden costs after the final BOQ is signed? +
Zero surprises. Our Bill of Quantities (BOQ) is 100% itemized by square footage, material brands, and hardware models. The price quoted upon final design sign-off is the exact price you pay at handover.
